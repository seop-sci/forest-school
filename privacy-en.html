Forest Animal School Privacy Policy

Forest Animal School (the "Game") is an idle game you can play without signing up. We do not ask for your name or phone number, and linking a Google account is optional. This page explains what information is processed while you play and why. If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version applies.

1. Information we process

InformationWhyWhere it is kept
Game progress (school stage, acorns, decorations, etc.) and an anonymous IDTo let you continue your gameOn your device and on Google Firebase servers.
Google account email and account identifier (only if you link an account)To continue the same school on a new phoneIn Google Firebase Authentication. Deleted on request.
Play statistics (progress stage, play time, number of sessions, number of ads watched)To improve game pacing and funOn Google Firebase servers under the anonymous ID.
Advertising ID, device information, approximate location (based on IP address), ad interaction dataTo show rewarded ads and measure ad performance (Google AdMob)Processed under Google's policies.
Previous schools (progress saved just before a school was replaced or overwritten, up to 3)To restore a school with Settings > Restore previous schoolOn your device and on Google Firebase servers under the anonymous ID. The oldest is replaced first.
Ranking name (only if you set one)To show you in the rankingDeleted when you leave the ranking.
Your school's look (building levels, decorations, student types, school name; only if "Show my school" is on)So other principals can visit your school from the rankingOn Google Firebase servers. Deleted when you turn sharing off or leave the ranking (if offline, when you reconnect).

2. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (ads): Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Firebase (server saves, Google account linking): Privacy and Security in Firebase

3. Turning off ad personalization

On Android, go to Settings > Google > All services > Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID. In places where the law requires it (for example, some US states), Google's privacy message in the Game lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

4. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Deleting your account and data

Delete in the Game (Android app): open the Settings tab, then tap Delete under School records > Delete account & server data. Tapping Delete all immediately deletes your school records on the server, previous-school slots, ranking name and score, your shared school look, play statistics, and your account (the anonymous ID and any linked Google account). The school and previous schools on this device are also deleted, and a new school starts.

If you linked a Google account, a Google sign-in window may appear once more for security. Server records are deleted first, then the account. If the connection drops before everything is deleted, deletion continues the next time you open the Game, and nothing new is saved to the server in the meantime. If records still remain, please email us at the address below.

Request deletion without the app: if you have already removed the app or cannot open the Game, email the developer address shown on the Game's Google Play page with the subject "Account deletion request". Include the email of your linked Google account or the principal name you used in the ranking, and we will delete the records above within 7 days and reply.

Deleted records cannot be restored. Ad-related information (Google AdMob) is handled under Google's policies, and you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone settings. Records stored only on your device are removed with Settings > Start over, or by uninstalling the app.

6. Contact

Please email the developer address shown on the Game's Google Play page.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page with its effective date.
